# Scientific Evaluation of Generated NeXus Files

**Evaluation date:** 2026-09-03

**Branch:** `experiment/metadata-mapping-benchmark`

**Review type:** AI-assisted technical and semantic review

> This report is not approval by a human experimental scientist. Its conclusions
> should be confirmed by appropriate microscopy, AFM, Raman, and NeXus experts
> before publication.

## 1. Evaluation scope

Seven NeXus files generated by the FAIR NeXus Metadata Mapper were evaluated
independently. Each generated file was compared with its selected local NXDL
application definition:

- `NXem.nxdl.xml` for the three electron-microscopy cases;
- `NXafm.nxdl.xml` for the two AFM cases;
- `NXraman.nxdl.xml` for the two Raman cases.

The review inspected the actual HDF5 datasets and attributes in each `.nxs` file,
including:

- `/entry/definition`;
- `/entry/source_image/image`;
- `/entry/mapping_provenance/data`;
- `/entry/unmapped_metadata/data`;
- `source_metadata_key` and `predicted_nxdl_path` attributes on mapped datasets.

Every written mapping was reviewed for:

1. existence in the selected NXDL definition;
2. whether the target is a writable field rather than a group;
3. scientific equivalence of the source and target concepts;
4. placement under the correct instrument component;
5. physical-quantity compatibility;
6. units;
7. datatype and shape;
8. required transformation or normalization;
9. target collisions and lost semantic information.

## 2. Verdict definitions

- **Correct:** The path, concept, component, physical meaning, datatype, shape,
  and units are compatible. Only trivial formatting may remain.
- **Partially correct:** The target is related, but conversion, normalization,
  array construction, unit assignment, vendor decoding, or more precise component
  placement is required.
- **Incorrect:** The path has the wrong scientific meaning, component, datatype,
  shape, or physical quantity.
- **Unverifiable:** The available metadata and NXDL documentation are insufficient
  to reach a defensible conclusion.

## 12. Required-field and completeness assessment

### NXem

The generated NXem files do not provide a complete sample description or the
required coordinate-system structure. Their `start_time` values are missing,
incomplete, or not valid ISO-8601 date-times. Consequently, none is a complete
NXem application-definition record.

### NXafm

The generated NXafm files lack required AFM fields and groups, notably
`scan_mode` and the experimental instrument structure. They cannot be considered
complete NXafm records.

### NXraman

The generated NXraman files lack `experiment_type`, `raman_experiment_type`, and
the Raman instrument and spectral-data structures. They cannot be considered
complete NXraman records.

## 13. Recurring failure modes

1. **Path existence without semantic correctness.** All written predicted paths
   exist, but many represent a different scientific concept.
2. **Missing units.** Physical values such as voltage, current, stage position,
   angle, and pixel spacing are written without unit attributes.
3. **Axis confusion.** Pixel counts and physical image extents are written as
   axis datasets without constructing calibrated coordinate arrays.
4. **Component confusion.** Stage, source, detector, aperture, and lens values
   are frequently placed under the wrong component.
5. **Fabrication misuse.** Numeric operating settings are mapped to textual
   fabrication vendor/model fields.
6. **Physical-quantity confusion.** Beam current is treated as emission current,
   and settings are treated as measured data.
7. **Datatype mismatch.** Boolean, integer, date-time, and identifier fields
   receive incompatible source representations.
8. **Target collisions.** Multiple source keys map to one NeXus field. The
   exporter preserves later values as unresolved JSON instead of overwriting.
9. **Generic-image limitation.** Rendered AFM and Raman TIFFs contain insufficient
   technique-specific metadata for meaningful NXafm or NXraman generation.
10. **Classification error.** The ZEISS SEM file is incorrectly identified as a
    TESCAN instrument.

## 14. Final conclusions

1. All seven files are structurally readable HDF5/NeXus files.
2. All 51 written predicted paths occur in their selected local NXDL definition.
3. Only 10 of the 51 written mappings are judged strictly correct in this review.
4. FEI Helios performs best, followed by TESCAN; ZEISS mapping is insufficient.
5. The AFM and Raman files preserve their images but do not contain meaningful
   technique-specific mapped metadata.
6. None of the generated files is complete enough to claim full NXem, NXafm, or
   NXraman application-definition compliance.
7. The mapper requires technique-aware parsers, unit handling, value conversion,
   datatype validation, collision resolution, required-field checks, and stronger
   semantic constraints before the generated files are suitable for publication.

## 15. Publication caveat

The counts and verdicts in this document are an AI-assisted interpretation of
the source metadata, generated files, and local NXDL definitions. A defensible
publication benchmark should use independently curated path-level ground truth
and review by experimental-domain and NeXus experts.
